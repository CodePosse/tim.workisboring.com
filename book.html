Install the bookmarklet

★Save to Research

Drag this button to Chrome's bookmarks bar. The same Research List tab will be reused while it remains open.

How to use it

  1. Show Chrome's bookmarks bar with Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows/Linux) or ⌘+Shift+B (Mac).
  2. Drag Save to Research onto the bookmarks bar.
  3. While browsing any page you want to save, click the bookmarklet.
  4. The page title, URL, description, and selected text are added to this Research List.
  5. Keep this tab open and future saves will reuse it instead of creating more Research List tabs.
